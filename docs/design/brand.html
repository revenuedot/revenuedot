Brand direction · RevenueDot

Monochrome infrastructure, one gold dot.

Black, white and neutral grey carry everything, in the grammar of Vercel, Profound and Resend. Gold is the only brand color, and it appears once per view: the mark, the live indicator, focus, and the one number that matters. Geist and Geist Mono throughout.

Mark

A full stop that is also a live light

The wordmark ends "revenue" with a gold dot. The app icon places that dot where a full stop sits. In the product the same dot pulses to mean "live".

Color

Neutrals do the work; gold marks the point

Primary buttons stay black on light and white on dark. Green, red and orange mean state only. Warning is orange so it never reads as the brand.

Why this direction

What it signals to each buyer

Developers

Geist, code-first hero, monochrome UI: it looks like the tools they already use every day.

Enterprise buyers

Calm near-black, restrained color, precise tables: the look of infrastructure that won't surprise them.

Revenue

Gold reads as money without taking chart green, which stays free to mean "up".

Distinct

No developer-infrastructure brand owns gold. Nothing echoes RevenueCat's red and indigo.
